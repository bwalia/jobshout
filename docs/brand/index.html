JobShout is mission control for AI teams. You say what needs doing; a team of agents plans it, runs it and reports back.

The Shout wheel

A hub (you) broadcasting to a team of agents. Four long spokes reach the orbit and four short ones sit inside it, so the wheel has a beat. The top-right agent is live.

  1. Hub with a cream core: you, the one giving the instruction.
  2. Four long spokes: the lead agents, out on the orbit.
  3. Four short spokes: specialists working closer in.
  4. The orbit, broken at each lead so it never cuts through a node: the team.
  5. The live agent, amber with a halo, always top right: something is running.

App icons

JobShout.app ships two masters. The Mac icon has the rounded-square body, rim and shadow baked in; the iPhone, iPad and Watch icon is full-bleed and the system masks it. Under 32 px the Mac uses a four-spoke wheel.

Sizes

The full wheel holds up to 32 px. Below that, the simplified wheel drops the orbit and short spokes.

Colour

The Shout gradient is for the mark, the "Shout" in the wordmark and hero moments, never for body text. Amber means live and nothing else.
